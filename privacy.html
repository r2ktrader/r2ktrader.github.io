Privacy Policy

MDR Research Sync reads only the Google Drive files its owner explicitly authorizes, using read-only access, and copies them to computers the owner controls. It never modifies, shares, sells or transmits that data to any third party, never uses it for advertising or any other purpose, and keeps the Google sign-in token only on the owner's own machine. The tool has a single user, its owner, and collects no information about anyone else. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions: michael.rodzik@gmail.com.
